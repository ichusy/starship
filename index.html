How to install

It takes a minute. The extra step is because StarShip is new and not yet certified by Apple or Microsoft.

On a Mac

  1. Download StarShip-Mac.zip and double-click it to unzip.
  2. Drag StarShip into your Applications folder.
  3. Open StarShip. macOS says it could not verify it: click Done.
  4. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to StarShip, then confirm.
  5. From now on, StarShip opens normally.

Needs a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later). Importing recordings needs macOS 26 or later.

On Windows

  1. Download StarShip-Windows.zip.
  2. Right-click it, choose Extract All…, and put the folder in your Documents.
  3. Open the folder and double-click StarShip.exe.
  4. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

Tip: right-click StarShip.exe › Show more options › Send to › Desktop to get a shortcut.

Your data stays yours

StarShip has no account and no server of its own.

  • Everything is stored on your computer, nowhere else.
  • You sign in on your university's own page: StarShip never sees your password.
  • Access keys are encrypted by your computer's secure storage.
  • StarShip only reads: it never submits, posts or changes anything.
  • AI summaries use your own Claude API key, sent only to Anthropic.
  • Remove an access, or erase everything, at any time in Settings.
